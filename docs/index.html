The Four Tenets of AI:
Cost, Bridge, Work, Theory

Each one has to be as low as possible.

Read the rulesGitHub repobound-loop
TENET 01

Cost

Money and compute per answer. Cheap answers scale; expensive ones don't.

Measure: $ / tokens per answer

TENET 02

Bridge

The glue to the real world — APIs, tools, translation layers. Every hop is a failure point.

Measure: integration hops per task

TENET 03

Work

Steps, retries, loops. Bounded and few — like bound-loop.

Measure: steps & retries per task

TENET 04

Theory

Unproven assumptions and unverified claims. Verify, or label it.

Measure: unverified claims

FAQ

What are the four tenets of AI?

Cost, Bridge, Work and Theory — each must be as low as possible.

What does Bridge mean?

The integration hops (APIs, tools, translation layers) between a model and the real world.

Why is Theory a cost?

Unverified assumptions are hidden debt that fails silently later.

Can I trade one tenet for another?

Only if the total goes down.
